PostPique

Share meaningful quotes from articles with your thoughts—directly to GitHub Pages.

PostPique makes it simple to capture and share interesting quotes from web articles along with your personal reflections. Connect your GitHub Pages repository and turn your reading into a curated collection of insights.

Coming Soon to the App Store

How to Use

  • 1️⃣ Select a GitHub Pages repository
  • 2️⃣ Open the share sheet on any webpage
  • 3️⃣ Choose PostPique
  • 4️⃣ Add a quote and your thoughts
  • 5️⃣ Post to GitHub Pages

Features

  • 📝 Quote any text from web articles
  • 💭 Add your personal thoughts and commentary
  • 🌐 Publish directly to your GitHub Pages site
  • 🔗 Automatic source attribution and linking
  • 📱 Works seamlessly with iOS and macOS share sheets
