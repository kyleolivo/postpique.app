Privacy Policy for PostPique

Effective Date: July 27, 2025

1. Data Collection

PostPique does not collect or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data related to your GitHub connection and app preferences is stored:

  • Locally on your device
  • On your device keychain

This data is private to you and is not accessible by the app developer or third parties.

2. No Third-Party Analytics or Tracking

PostPique does not use any third-party analytics, advertising services, or tracking technologies. Your app usage data is never shared or monitored.

3. GitHub Integration

PostPique connects to GitHub to publish content to your GitHub Pages repository. Your GitHub authentication is handled directly by GitHub and we do not store your GitHub credentials. The app only accesses repositories you explicitly authorize.

4. Data Deletion

Because PostPique stores data only on your device and in your iCloud account, deleting the app or removing it from iCloud will remove your data. You are fully in control of your data at all times.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you may contact the developer.
